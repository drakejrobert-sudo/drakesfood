# Drew's Mexican Street Corn

A smoky, creamy, slightly charred Mexican street corn-style side inspired by Drew's Mother's Day taco bar corn.

## Submitted By

Drew

Recipe inspiration credit goes to Drew.

## Original Inspiration

Drew made a creamy Mexican street corn-style topping in bulk for a family taco bar. This version keeps that creamy corn-dish format while adding grilled corn, smoky char, warm spices, hot sauce, and optional Tajín.

## Ingredients

- 6 ears fresh corn
- Mayonnaise, for brushing the corn and making the sauce
- 2 knobs butter
- Sour cream, to taste
- Chili powder, to taste
- Paprika, to taste
- Cayenne pepper, to taste
- Garlic powder, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- Hot sauce, to taste
- Tajín, for topping, optional

## Instructions

1. Brush the corn with a light coating of mayonnaise.
2. Grill or smoke the corn at about 350°F for 15 to 20 minutes, rotating occasionally, until cooked and lightly charred.
3. Slice the kernels off the cobs.
4. Melt the butter in a pot over medium heat.
5. Add the corn kernels to the pot.
6. Season with black pepper, garlic powder, chili powder, paprika, and cayenne.
7. Cook until the corn is fragrant and warmed through.
8. Stir in mayonnaise and sour cream until the corn is creamy and reaches your preferred texture.
9. Add a squirt of hot sauce and cook, stirring occasionally, until hot.
10. Serve as a side, taco bar topping, or bed for taquitos.
11. Finish with Tajín if desired.

## Notes

Amounts are intentionally flexible. Start lighter with the mayo, sour cream, spices, and hot sauce, then adjust until the corn tastes creamy, smoky, spicy, and a little tangy.

The grilled corn adds the biggest flavor upgrade. A few flare-ups are not required, but they did help add some extra char.
